## ✨ Overview

**Travelio** brings travel discovery and personal reservations together in a clean, native Android experience. Explore places to stay, discover trip highlights, browse transportation, and keep your reservations in one place.

With a dark interface, vivid accents, and smooth transitions, every screen puts the journey first.

## 🧭 Features

| | Explore with Travelio |
| :---: | :--- |
| 🏨 | **Find your next stay** — Browse hotels, ratings, nightly prices, and amenities. |
| 🌍 | **Discover new experiences** — Explore trips, destination details, durations, and activity highlights. |
| ✈️ | **Choose your way there** — Browse plane, bus, boat, and car options with route and departure information. |
| 🎟️ | **Save your plans** — Select your guest count, preview the total, and save a reservation to your account. |
| 🗂️ | **Keep everything together** — View current and expired reservations in dedicated tabs. |
| 🔐 | **Make it yours** — Sign in with Google or email, update your name, and recover your password. |

## 🎨 The experience

- 🌙 **A distinctive dark interface** with navy backgrounds and cyan and blue accents.
- 🖼️ **Image-led discovery** with rich cards and expanding detail headers.
- 💫 **Smooth motion** through shared image transitions and animated lists.
- ⚡ **Thoughtful feedback** with shimmer loading, refresh gestures, and clear empty and error states.
- ✅ **A simple reservation flow** with guest selection, instant totals, and animated confirmation.
